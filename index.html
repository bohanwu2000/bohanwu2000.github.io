About

I am a Ph.D. student in Statistics at Columbia University, advised by David Blei. Before that, I received a B.S. in Mathematics and Statistics from Rice University, where I worked with Daniel Kowal.

I design methods and theory for large-scale probabilistic inference. See my papers. My recent interests are:

  • Scaling and generalizing empirical Bayes
  • Sampling from the posterior via optimal transport
  • Learning causality from heterogeneous data
